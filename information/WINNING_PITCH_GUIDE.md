## 🎪 **Q&A STRATEGY**

### **Expected Questions & Responses:**

**Q: "How is this different from existing satellite communication?"**
**A:** "Current systems lose data during blackouts. We're the first to guarantee zero data loss through intelligent store-and-forward buffering and satellite mesh networking."

**Q: "What's your technical innovation?"**
**A:** "We combine three innovations: intelligent buffering algorithms, satellite-to-satellite relay networking, and AI-powered link selection. No one else has integrated these for zero data loss."

**Q: "How do you validate the $50M savings claim?"**
**A:** "We analyzed documented mission failures like Luna-25 ($50M), NASA communication losses, and ESA mission costs. Our system prevents these specific failure modes."

**Q: "What's your go-to-market strategy?"**
**A:** "We target space agencies first - NASA, ESA, JAXA - who have documented communication problems. Then commercial operators like SpaceX and satellite constellation providers."

**Q: "How scalable is this solution?"**
**A:** "Extremely scalable. Our mesh networking approach actually improves with more satellites. Each new satellite becomes a potential relay node, increasing system reliability."

**Q: "What are the technical limitations?"**
**A:** "Latency increases with satellite relay (250-350ms vs 20-50ms direct). However, zero data loss is more valuable than slightly higher latency for mission-critical applications."
